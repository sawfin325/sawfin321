Chrono24 Buyer Protection

Eligible purchases are paid into escrow, checked against the listing, and covered by a money-back promise.

Payment via the Escrow Service

The buyer pays Chrono24, not the seller. We hold the funds until the watch arrives and the buyer confirms it matches the listing. Only then is the seller paid, minus a small commission.

Commitment to Authenticity

Dealers agree to authenticity guidelines before they list. Certified watches can be inspected by independent specialists. Counterfeit listings are removed, and repeat violations end in a ban.
