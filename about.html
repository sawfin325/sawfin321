About this Chrono24 demo

This is a static HTML recreation of the Chrono24 marketplace layout, including the dark header and Buy a watch mega menu. Gauri is a sample maison with a full product line listed for purchase in the catalogue.

It is a front-end demo: listings are sample data, checkout does not take payment, and accounts are stored only in your browser.
