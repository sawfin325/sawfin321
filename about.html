About Meridian

Meridian is a static HTML recreation of a global luxury-watch marketplace. The homepage, search, listings, magazine, and security pages follow the public structure of chrono24.com — with original photography, copy, and branding.

It is a front-end demo: listings are sample data, checkout does not take payment, and accounts are stored only in your browser.
