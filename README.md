# luxerywatchsales

An original luxury-watch marketplace demo. Open `index.html` in a browser.

luxerywatchsales is a fictional catalogue: original maisons, menus, listings, and copy. It is not affiliated with any commercial watch marketplace.

## Pages

- Homepage video strip (Lights, Camera, Action) with playable films, plus four buying guides
- 50,000 listings on each of 48 original maisons (2,400,000 watches in the catalogue)
- 1,200 listings in each of 18 shop rooms; Buy a watch lists every maison
- Each listing has an 8-image gallery (studio photo plus seven detail views)
- Footer: buy/sell/about, payments, country flags for 48 regions, legal
- Contact: +1 913-278-5312 and luxerywatchsales@gmail.com on Help, Contact, listings, and the footer
- Orders go out on WhatsApp or email (no card checkout)
- Collectors can create an account and log in on the site
