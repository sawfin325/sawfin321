Frequently asked questions

How does Buyer Protection work?

You pay Chrono24. We hold the money while the seller ships. After you receive the watch and it matches the listing, we pay the seller.

Are the watches authentic?

Dealers agree to authenticity rules. Certified listings can be inspected. If a watch is not as described, eligible orders are refunded.

Who pays for shipping?

Each listing states shipping terms. Most professional dealers offer insured worldwide shipping; the cost may be included or added at checkout.

Can I return a watch?

Easy returns apply to eligible Buyer Protection orders when the watch does not match the listing. See the listing page for the seller’s own return policy.

How do I sell my watch?

Open Sell a watch, enter the model, photos, and a price. Private sellers and dealers use the same listing flow in this demo.

Is this the real Chrono24 website?

No. This is an original static HTML demo inspired by Chrono24’s public layout. It is not affiliated with Chrono24 GmbH and does not process real payments.
