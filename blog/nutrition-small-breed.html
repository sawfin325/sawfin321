Feeding small-breed puppies the right way

Pomeranian puppies have tiny stomachs and fast metabolisms. Skipping meals can be dangerous, especially in very small pups.

What we send home

We start puppies on a named small-breed puppy kibble. Keep that food for at least two weeks, then transition slowly if you change brands. Measure with a scale, not a giant scoop.

Treats

Training treats should be pea-sized. Cheese and table scraps add up. If the waist disappears, cut treats first, not meals.

Water and hypoglycemia

Fresh water always. If a young puppy becomes wobbly, weak, or glassy-eyed, this is an emergency. Rub a little honey on the gums and call your vet. We also welcome a call at +1 743-259-3337.
